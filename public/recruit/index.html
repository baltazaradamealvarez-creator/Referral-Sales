The quick answers 03 / FAQ

Curious?
Good.

Here are a few things people usually want to know before they start.

What would I be selling?+

You’ll speak with customers about Spectrum internet and mobile services and help them explore available options. Product availability can vary by location.

Where does the selling happen?+

This is an in-person retail opportunity. Sales can happen at approved store locations and other community retail settings, depending on the program.

Do I need sales experience?+

Experience can help, but a friendly, dependable person who enjoys talking with people can learn. Training and guidance are part of getting started.

How does commission work?+

The opportunity includes uncapped commission potential for eligible sales. We’ll walk you through the current rates, eligibility, payment terms and requirements before you decide.

Is this an official Spectrum job?+

This page describes an opportunity with an independent authorized Spectrum retailer. It is not employment with Charter Communications or Spectrum. The exact business relationship and role terms will be shared before onboarding.
